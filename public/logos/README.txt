Company Logo Instructions
=========================

Place your company logo images in this folder with the following names:

1. sabre.png - Sabre Systems LLC logo
2. bessemer.png - Bessemer Trust logo
3. broadridge.png - Broadridge Financial Solutions logo

Recommended specifications:
- Format: PNG (with transparent background preferred)
- Size: Square images work best (e.g., 500x500px or 1000x1000px)
- The images will be automatically resized to fit the frame

The logos will appear in a photo frame in the top left corner of each experience card.
